TAARU Insights (Targeted Analytics Advisory & Research Unit) is a boutique research and advisory firm built on more than 12 years of deal and investment research experience. We work with private equity and venture capital investors, family offices and boutique investment banks on the analysis that drives investment decisions.

TAARU is a specialist research house, built for the workstreams that move a deal forward: target screening, valuation, commercial diligence, market and competitive intelligence, and IC-ready transaction materials.

We shape every engagement around the mandate: the founder directs it from the first call to final delivery, and we resource it to the scale the work demands.

The Founder

No Hand-Offs. No Understudies.

Nitant Kumar Arora

Founder

Nitant Kumar Arora is the founder of TAARU Insights and leads every engagement personally. He brings more than 12 years of experience in deal and investment research. Through TAARU, he works with private equity and venture capital investors, family offices, boutique investment banks and corporate strategy teams.

His expertise spans financial modeling and valuation, market and competitive intelligence, benchmarking and transaction analysis. He applies it where investment decisions are made: M&A, market entry, growth strategy and investment evaluation.

Nitant has led end-to-end research engagements across data centers and AI infrastructure, FIG and Fintech, energy (including oil and gas, power and utilities, and cleantech) and TMT, working directly with senior deal and strategy teams. His work supports opportunity screening, commercial due diligence and investment committee discussions, and produces decision-ready materials: financial models, market assessments, competitive analyses, pitchbooks and investment memoranda.

His approach is hands-on and structured. He turns fragmented market data into clear, defensible insight a deal team can act on. Nitant holds an Executive MBA from IMT Ghaziabad and a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of Delhi.

Specialist Services, Built Around the Deal Cycle

We understand the rhythm of deal evaluation — the speed required, the depth demanded and the precision an investment committee expects.

Opportunity Screening & Pipeline Research

Target identification, market sizing, whitespace analysis and comparable-transaction benchmarking, enabling a clear go/no-go view before committing diligence time and cost.

Commercial Diligence Research

Independent market, competitor and customer analysis on a live deal, covering market size and growth, competitive positioning, key risks and the priority questions for management, structured to support the investment committee decision.

Valuation & Investment Case

A valuation you can defend in committee: DCF, trading comparables and precedent transactions reconciled into one range, with the sensitivities that move it, and a memo that ties the thesis and key risks to the numbers.

Portfolio Monitoring & Value Creation

Ongoing benchmarking of portfolio companies against peers, covering financial and operational KPIs, market and competitor developments, and the evidence behind value creation plans across the hold period, through to exit preparation.
